A Library, Not a Sales Pitch

Our job is to help you make a confident decision, not to sell you one. Most of what's below isn't ours: it's the books and free guides we keep coming back to on downsizing, estate planning, aging in place, caregiving, and everything in between.

Bookmark this page. New sections get added as we find things worth sharing.

The Gentle Art of Swedish Death Cleaning

One of the most recommended books on this list. Despite the title, it's really about simplifying life and lightening the load you leave behind for the people who love you.

Downsizing the Family HomeMarni Jameson

Downsizing the Family Home: What to Save, What to Let Go

Marni Jameson

Written for both sides of the conversation: the parent letting go of a house, and the adult child helping them do it without a fight.

Find this book

Not written with seniors in mind, but the decision-making method (does this still serve you) is one a lot of our clients end up using anyway.
