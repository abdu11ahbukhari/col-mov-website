Senior Real Estate Specialist · SRES®

Valerie Van Pelt

Valerie spent twenty-five years in sales for companies like Medtronic, Boston Scientific, and Johnson & Johnson, often in rooms where a decision mattered as much as a cardiac surgery. She brought that same weight of attention into real estate, and it didn't take long for people to notice: her first year in the business, she closed a multi-million-dollar sale.

It's the SRES® certification, though, that changed the kind of client she works with. A grown daughter calling about her father's house from three states away isn't a listing to Valerie. It's a family trying to do right by someone who raised them. She's staged a home for sale within days of a difficult family loss, negotiated around a lender's last-minute denial without losing the deal, and spent seven patient months house-hunting with a client who needed someone willing to wait for the right one.

Within Collective Moves, Valerie is usually the first call: the one who sees the whole picture of the house and the family's timeline, and knows exactly who else on the team needs to be looped in before a For Sale sign ever goes in the yard.

Downsizing, Space Planning & Moving

Lori Olson

Lori handles the part everyone dreads: forty years of belongings and a floor plan half the size. She works room by room with a family to decide what's kept, what's sold, what's donated, and what goes to which grandchild, at a pace the person actually moving can live with.

Before moving day she measures the new space and plans it out, so the sofa, the bed, and the pieces that matter are known to fit before anything is loaded on a truck. Then she manages the move itself: packing, the movers, and unpacking on the other end, so the first night in the new place already feels like home rather than a room full of boxes.

Lori comes in early, alongside the realtor. Sorting and packing on her schedule is what lets a house be photographed, listed, and shown without a scramble, and it keeps the move itself from landing on the family all at once.
Estate Planning Attorney

Aaron Miller

Aaron handles the legal architecture underneath every transition: wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and the probate questions that tend to surface at the worst possible time. He works alongside a family's existing attorney when there is one, and steps in directly when there isn't.

His job is to make sure a home sale, a move, or a change in care doesn't quietly create a legal complication nobody saw coming, whether that's an outdated beneficiary, an unsigned power of attorney, or a title issue buried in old paperwork.

Aaron reviews the legal side of a transaction in step with the rest of the team's timeline, so a closing date is never held up by paperwork that could have been handled months earlier.
