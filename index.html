What Usually Happens

Most Families Assemble This Team Anyway, Just in the Wrong Order

A parent falls, or a diagnosis changes everything, or someone finally says the thing everyone's been thinking privately for months. Suddenly there's a house to deal with, a will that hasn't been touched in a decade, and three siblings trying to agree on a senior living community none of them have actually toured.

Normally, you find each of these people separately: a realtor from a yard sign, an attorney from a search result, a moving company from a flyer on the fridge. Somewhere in the middle of it, you become the one holding all the pieces together, retelling the same story to every new person you call.

Collective Moves exists because holding it all together shouldn't fall on you.

One Call, Multiple Experts

One Conversation Starts All of It

Make the call, and you're not just hiring a real estate agent. You're opening a line to everyone your family is likely to need over the next few months, introduced in the right order, without you managing the handoffs yourself.

I.

SRES® Realtor

A REALTOR® trained specifically in the realities of a senior sale: staging around mobility needs, timing a closing around a care transition, and pricing a home that may not have been appraised in thirty years.

II.

Financial Planning Resources

Referrals to financial professionals who can answer the harder question underneath the sale: does this plan actually fund the next ten years, and what happens if it needs to fund twenty?

III.

Elder Law & Estate Planning

A wills-trusts-and-probate attorney who makes sure the sale of a home doesn't leave a legal tangle behind for whoever inherits what's left.

IV.

Senior Living Navigation

Someone who has actually walked the halls of the communities being considered, and can tell a family honestly which ones are worth the drive to tour.

V.

Downsizing, Space Planning & Moving

A plan for decades of belongings: what's sold, what's donated, what's kept, and who in the family gets to decide what. The new place is measured and laid out first, so everything that comes along is known to fit.

VI.

Senior Moving

A crew that specializes in moving people who've lived in one place a long time, not just moving boxes, but moving a life with some care.
